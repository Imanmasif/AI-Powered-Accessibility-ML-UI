# Limitations and Future Work

The paper identifies several limitations and directions for further research.

## Current limitations

- The present scope is limited to colour contrast rather than the full accessibility space.
- Highly branded or tightly constrained interfaces may resist even small visual changes.
- User-specific perceptual needs vary, so a single threshold cannot represent every user equally well.
- Broader validation is needed across accessibility conditions, interfaces and populations.

## Future directions

The paper proposes extending AdaptiveUI toward:

- user-specific calibration using behavioural feedback, gaze information or adjustment overrides
- semantic-aware optimisation using design-system or ARIA information
- typography and text-size adaptation
- spacing and layout adaptation
- semantic hierarchy preservation
- motion reduction
- alternative-text and other accessibility support
- richer user controls for acceptable visual change

The broader direction is a hybrid accessibility model in which established design-time conformance practices are complemented by intelligent client-side adaptation during real interaction.
